# Medieval Fantasy – Text-Based RPG

## About the Game
Medieval Fantasy is a console-based role-playing game developed in **C#** as a group project.

The player is transported into a mysterious fantasy world called **Ogatoberg**, where they must explore, complete quests, defeat enemies, and challenge four powerful bosses to find their way home.

## Features
- **Character Creation:** Choose Warrior, Rogue, Mage, or customize your own stats.
- **Turn-Based Combat:** Attack, cast spells, use items, or escape encounters.
- **Enemy Progression:** 20 normal enemies divided into four stages, unlocked by defeating bosses.
- **Boss Battles:** Fight the Undead Twins, the Paladin, the Warlock, and Godghetti.
- **Character Progression:** Earn experience, level up, and improve attributes.
- **Inventory and Equipment:** Collect items, equip weapons and armour, and purchase supplies.
- **Quest System:** Complete main and side quests to progress through the story.
- **Save and Load:** Save your character information and quest progress.

## How to Run
1. Open the project in Visual Studio.
2. Ensure `enemyList.txt` and `bossList.txt` are in their expected locations.
3. Build and run the C# console application.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to begin your adventure.

## Development Status
This game is currently in development. Core gameplay systems are implemented, while additional enemy abilities, combat balancing, and improvements will be introduced following playtesting.
